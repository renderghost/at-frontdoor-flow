New to the Atmosphere

Jo Park · Has never used an Atmosphere app. Arrives from a friend’s recipe.

  1. PotluckLands on Potluck, signed out.
  2. PotluckPresses “Create an Atmosphere account”.
  3. Sign in with AtmosphereThe sign-in checks this device for an existing account.
  4. Sign in with AtmosphereNone is found, so Jo carries on.
  5. Sign in with AtmosphereChooses a provider. Potluck is suggested first, then everyone else A to Z.
  6. ProviderCreates the account at the provider with an email and a password.
  7. ProviderChooses a handle, with a suggestion already filled in.
  8. ProviderConsent, as a short summary: the account was just made for Potluck.
  9. PotluckSigned in. Potluck’s welcome explains the one account.

Knows their details

Sam Okafor · Has a Bluesky account and knows it, on a laptop the browser doesn’t know.

  1. PotluckLands on Potluck, signed out.
  2. PotluckPresses “Continue with Atmosphere”.
  3. PotluckThe browser doesn’t know an Atmosphere account yet.
  4. Sign in with AtmosphereTypes sam, @sam.bsky.social or sam@bsky.social. Suggestions appear.
  5. Sign in with AtmosphereThe account is found.
  6. Sign in with AtmosphereSees which site comes next, and why: Bluesky.
  7. ProviderSigns in on Bluesky’s own page. Only the password is left to type.
  8. ProviderConsent: connects Potluck to the account.
  9. PotluckSigned in. Next time the browser offers this account, and Sam goes straight in.

Forgot their details

Rae Thompson · Made an account with Blacksky last year. Remembers only the email address.

  1. PotluckLands on Potluck, signed out.
  2. PotluckPresses “Continue with Atmosphere”.
  3. PotluckThe browser doesn’t know an Atmosphere account yet.
  4. Sign in with AtmosphereCan’t remember the handle.
  5. Sign in with AtmospherePicks “Forgot your handle?” to use the email address instead.
  6. Sign in with AtmosphereEnters the email address and picks Blacksky, or “Not sure”.
  7. Sign in with AtmosphereTold who will email, and that it can take a minute.
  8. InboxBlacksky’s email arrives with the forgotten handle and a one-time code.
  9. ProviderThe link opens Blacksky in a new tab with the code filled in. Signs in.
  10. ProviderOffered a new password, and can choose “Not now”.
  11. ProviderConsent, in the Blacksky tab.
  12. PotluckThe tab closes. Signed in to Potluck.

Has one, doesn’t know it

Priya Shah · Uses Bluesky, and assumes Potluck needs a new account.

  3. Sign in with AtmosphereThe sign-in checks this device before anything is created.

Reading the diagram

  • Start: lands on Potluck
  • A step or screen
  • A decision. Each way out carries a guard, like [yes]
  • A merge, where two ways in meet
  • A connector: carries on at the other A, consent in a new tab
  • End: signed in to Potluck

Also in the prototype

Side routes, left out of the drawing to keep it readable.

  • The browser’s list offers “Use another Atmosphere account”, which goes to finding the account.
  • Finding the account offers “New here? Create an Atmosphere account”.
  • Creating an account offers “Search for yourself”, back to finding it.
  • “Who we found” has a back button to finding the account.
  • At the provider’s sign-in, “Forgot password?” emails a code to sign in with.
  • “Check your email” offers “Try another provider” and “Search by name instead”.
  • Cancelling consent, or closing the sign-in, goes back to Potluck’s sign-in page.
  • A new account gets a welcome email. It doesn’t hold up signing in.
